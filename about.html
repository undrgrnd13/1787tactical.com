About, FFL & fees

1787 TACTICAL, LLC. is a Type 07 FFL licensed manufacturer in Loxahatchee, Florida. We sell and transfer firearms in person only, by appointment.

Type 07 FFL

We are a federally licensed Type 07 manufacturer. For public trust we state our license class only. The full FFL license number is not published on this website; it is provided to transferring FFLs on request.

Our license is current (expires November 1, 2026). Always verify receiving FFL details through standard industry channels before shipping a firearm to us for transfer.

How buying works

  1. Call or text to confirm inventory and schedule an appointment.
  2. Arrive at the scheduled time with valid ID.
  3. Complete ATF Form 4473 and FDLE background check on site.
  4. Pay in person (cash, Zelle, or Venmo) and take possession when approved.

We do not ship firearms directly to individuals.

Compliance

  • All transfers follow ATF, Florida, and local requirements.
  • ATF Form 4473 and FDLE firearm purchase background checks are required.
  • If a transaction is non-approved, FDLE provides an appeal window (typically 60 days). Ask us about next steps and any return/shipping fees.
